# Topic: The 2026 email client landscape and its gaps

Survey current clients as of 2026-10-11 (verify versions, prices and
features on official sites): Apple Mail (macOS 26), Mimestream, Spark,
Superhuman, HEY, Fastmail's apps, Proton Mail desktop, Thunderbird
(desktop and iOS), new Outlook for Mac/Windows, Canary Mail, Airmail,
MailMate, Notion Mail, Shortwave, Missive, Front, eM Client, Mailspring,
Postbox (status?), Geary/Evolution/KMail on Linux, and anything notable
launched in 2025-2026.

Questions:
1. For each: platforms, tech stack (native, Electron, web), business
   model and price, backends supported, standout features, privacy model
   (do they proxy mail through their servers?), AI features.
2. Recurring complaints in reviews and forums (Hacker News, Reddit
   r/macapps, MacRumors, TidBITS, App Store reviews): what frustrates
   power users and ordinary users today?
3. Which features do power users ask for that nobody delivers well
   (e.g. true native speed with server-agnostic support, great search,
   powerful rules, plain-text and Markdown composing, scripting, keyboard
   control, local-first privacy, unified Exchange + IMAP + Proton)?
4. AI in email in 2026: what is useful (summaries, triage, drafting,
   semantic search) and what users reject; on-device options on macOS
   26 (Apple Foundation Models framework, Writing Tools) vs cloud.
5. Where is the opening for Sweetbriar? Who is the target user, what is
   the positioning, and what must it do better than Apple Mail and
   Mimestream to justify switching?
