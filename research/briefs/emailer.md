# Topic: Claris Emailer (classic Mac email client)

Leads to check, not facts to repeat: Claris Emailer (mid-1990s), its
relationship to Fog City Software, connections to America Online and
CompuServe as well as POP/SMTP, scheduled connections ("Schedules"),
"Actions" (rules), offline-first design for dial-up, a unified view of
multiple services, address book, AppleScript support, Emailer 2.0, the
end of Claris (FileMaker) and Emailer's discontinuation, and any lineage
to Outlook Express / Entourage for Mac. Find manuals, Claris press
releases, Macworld/MacUser/TidBITS reviews, and reminiscences of people
who still describe it as the best Mac mail client.
