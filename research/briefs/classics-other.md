# Topic: Other beloved classic email clients and what modern clients lost

Survey beloved older or niche clients beyond Eudora, Mailsmith, Claris
Emailer and Musashi: Mulberry (IMAP power client), Pine/Alpine, Mutt,
Gnus, The Bat!, Pegasus Mail, Outlook Express, Netscape Messenger,
Opera M2 (views instead of folders), PowerMail (Mac), Microsoft Entourage,
Lotus Notes, Becky! Internet Mail (Japan), early Apple Mail / NeXTMail,
Sparrow, Mailbox (swipe gestures, snooze), Google Inbox (bundles,
reminders), Postbox, MailMate (modern but keyboard and Markdown centric),
Mail Act-On / MailTags plug-ins, and others you find.

For each: the one to five features users most loved, with sources. Then
mine discussions of what people miss today (Hacker News threads such as
"Ask HN: best email client", Reddit r/macapps, TidBITS, MPU, blogs) and
produce a ranked catalog of "lost features" with: description, which
clients had it, why it disappeared, whether modern clients have an
equivalent, and how Sweetbriar could revive it on macOS in 2026.
