# Topic: Mailsmith (Mac email client)

Leads to check, not facts to repeat: Bare Bones Software (makers of BBEdit)
released Mailsmith in the late 1990s; it used BBEdit's text engine and was
loved by plain-text purists; later versions were handed to Stick Software
and released as freeware (mailsmith.org); AppleScript support; powerful
filters; Mail-handling with "personalities"; glossary/text factories,
grep search, mailbox structure, spam handling (SpamSieve integration?),
and its strong community (Mailsmith-Talk mailing list). Find the manuals,
release notes and version history (1.x, 2.x), the Bare Bones and
mailsmith.org pages on the Wayback Machine, TidBITS and Macworld reviews,
and reminiscences. Explain why text-centric users loved it and which of
its BBEdit-derived editing features are absent from mail clients today.
