# Topic: Musashi (Japanese Mac email client)

Start from https://macintoshgarden.org/apps/musashi (if HTTPS fails try
http:// or the Wayback Machine). Leads to check, not facts to repeat: a
less known email client from Japan by Sonosoft, for classic Mac OS and
possibly Mac OS X. Search in Japanese as well as English (for example
"Musashi メーラー", "ムサシ メール Mac", "Sonosoft Musashi", "Musashi
Mac OS 9 メールソフト"), Japanese Mac sites and magazines (MacFan,
MacPeople, ASCII, Impress Watch, Vector software library vector.co.jp,
mac.watch.impress.co.jp), old Sonosoft pages on the Wayback Machine, and
any English-speaking users. Describe its distinctive design ideas and
features, especially anything unusual compared with Western clients
(for example handling of Japanese encodings, threading, filtering,
mailbox organisation, UI). If you find the download archive or a manual,
describe its contents; translate Japanese material faithfully and mark
translations as such.
