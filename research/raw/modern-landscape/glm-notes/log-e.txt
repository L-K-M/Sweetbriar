**Key findings:**

- **Complaints cluster around four themes:** broken search (Apple Mail tokenization bugs, Thunderbird's Gloda finding nothing), sync rot (Mail/Exchange stalls, New Outlook "Updating Inbox" hangs and dropped local-IMAP support, Spark cross-device desync), monetization resentment ($30/mo Superhuman, $50/yr Mimestream, Spark paywalling block/unsubscribe), and lock-in (HEY has no IMAP, Mimestream/Notion are Gmail-only, New Outlook/Spark/Notion route mail through vendor clouds).
- **The big unmet gap:** no client combines native speed, IMAP+Exchange+Gmail+Proton coverage, Gmail-grade search, and offline/local-first operation. Sieve/server-rule editing, WYSIWYG-optional Markdown composing, and real scripting are essentially unserved — indie entrants (Marco, Penguin Mail, Talanoa) explicitly pitch this gap.
- **AI verdict:** users accept opt-in triage/auto-labels and thread summaries (Spark Smart Inbox, Notion auto-label praised) but reject forced bundling (Workspace Gemini non-disableable without support call), silent inbox-scanning, and unasked drafting. Apple's news-summary suspension after the BBC error is the canonical accuracy-backlash case.
- **macOS 26 on-device AI is the opportunity:** the ~3B/2-bit Foundation Models framework is free, offline, keyless, with guided generation and custom LoRA adapters — making private local AI email features table-stakes and neutralizing the cloud-AI privacy objection.
