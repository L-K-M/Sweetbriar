# M1.6q owner qualification: Search timings

Run the core timing runner on your Mac and record the dated result here
against spec/06's budgets. The archive is synthetic (seeded, 100,000
messages), so the report holds no mail of yours; keep your own queries
and addresses out of this public file. M1.6q2 adds the app's
last-keystroke to first rows timing against spec/08.

## Core timings (spec/11)

On a release build, with the Mac otherwise idle:

```sh
SB_SEARCH_TIMINGS_OUT=~/search-timings.json SB_RUSTC_VERSION="$(rustc -V)" \
  cargo test -p sb-engine --release --lib search_timings -- --ignored --nocapture
```

The run imports the archive through the real pipeline (expect it to
take a while), then times first-50 Search pages cold and warm, idle and
during background imports, exact counts, coverage, commit-to-searchable
freshness and a full rebuild. Failures are counted, never dropped.

| Budget (spec/06, 100k messages) | Report field | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Cached exact search p95 ≤ 100 ms during background sync | `workloads[background-import].warm.queries[exact-*].first50.p95` | met: 63.7 ms worst (exact-reference, relevance) |
| Cached exact search p99 ≤ 250 ms during background sync | `workloads[background-import].warm.queries[exact-*].first50.p99` | met: 105.9 ms worst (exact-reference, relevance) |
| New mail searchable within 2 s at p95 | `freshness.background-import.ms.p95` | met: 472 ms |
| Index rebuild ≤ 15 min | `rebuild.ms` | met: 57 s |

Also note the idle and cold figures, `counts` (exact unknown-count
time, spec/07) and the environment block (Mac model, macOS, SQLite).
A missed budget is a finding to resolve, not a threshold to relax.

Result: 2026-10-28, b1be23d (the report's commit field is unrecorded:
it reads `GITHUB_SHA`), Apple M3 Pro (Mac15,6, 12 cores), macOS 27.2,
SQLite 3.53.2, Homebrew rustc 1.99.1. All four budgets met. Notes:
the import took 35 min. Idle warm first-50 p95 over all queries was
94.5 ms, and 138 ms during imports. The broad queries are the slow
ones: common-term relevance had p95 177 ms idle and 210 ms during
imports, phrase and negative-body relevance about 95 ms idle. Cold
first-50 p50 ranged from 84 to 187 ms. Exact counts took 101 to 246 ms
at p50.
