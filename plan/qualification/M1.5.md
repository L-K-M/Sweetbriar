# M1.5 owner qualification: reader

Run these on your Mac with real imported mail and the CI-built app
(the `Sweetbriar-app` artifact). Record the date, commit and each result
here. Keep message content and private addresses out of this public file.
The synthetic CI tests cannot judge fidelity against your real mail.

## Reading imported mail

1. Open several HTML messages, including tables, long messages and
   non-ASCII text. Sender layout is readable and looks right.
2. Open plain messages, including nested quotes and format=flowed mail.
   Text stays literal, quote depths are clear, and scrolling works.
3. Open messages with inline CID images. Images belong to that message;
   missing or damaged images do not reveal another message's content.
4. Hover links and check the real destination. Follow a safe HTTP/HTTPS
   link: it opens in your default browser, leaving the reader in place.
   Check the warnings for a raw IP destination, user info in a URL and
   link text whose domain differs from its destination. Cancel a warning
   and confirm nothing opens; accept one and confirm the destination.
5. Open attachments with ordinary filenames and types. The right file
   opens externally; downloaded executable content remains quarantined
   and Gatekeeper applies. Do not bypass a warning to complete this check.
6. Toggle View Source and Sender Layout for HTML and plain messages.
   Source shows the raw message literally, and returning restores the body.
7. With Little Snitch or `nettop` watching the app and WebKit's network
   process, display messages containing remote images and styles.
   Nothing remote loads automatically, including after waiting and
   switching away and back. Do not click a link during this observation.
8. Switch quickly between HTML, plain and image-bearing messages, then
   clear the selection and select several rows. No previous body or inline
   image appears in the new selection; each final message stays correct.

Result: _date, commit, pass or fail per step, notes_
