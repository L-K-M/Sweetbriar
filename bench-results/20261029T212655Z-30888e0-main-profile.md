## Profiled run (numbers are not results)

`-profiled.json`: `ok` true, 50 of 50, p95 4,642 ms, p50 2,503 ms. The
profiler more than doubles the times. The analysis is in
`20261029T212655Z-30888e0-main-profile-profile.md`. In short:

- The main thread is on CPU for 11% of the search phase.
- The wait is SQLite page reads on the tokio workers:
  `read_search_relevance_page`, `search_ids` and `read_page`, with 55%
  of worker time in `pread`.
- `sb_store::sidebar::mailboxes` recounts during the search phase and
  takes 23.5% of worker time.
- On the main thread, the time goes to Core Animation commit and
  layout, `AppKitPrototypeList` row views, and
  `MessageListViewController`'s list swap.

## Anything unusual

- **Do Not Disturb:** on, per the human, who also agreed to leave the
  Mac alone. I couldn't verify it myself: the sandbox denied reading
  `~/Library/DoNotDisturb`. `screensharingd` was running, so a Screen
  Sharing session may have been connected.
- **First launch on this archive:** run 1 was the first time the app
  opened the freshly seeded archive. `~/sweetbriar-search-archive/cache`
  didn't exist until that launch created it (the seed recipe doesn't
  make it). Run 1's first round may include first-open work.
- **Build:** 516 s. `scripts/build.sh` modified
  `apps/macos/Sweetbriar/Info.plist` (one line) in the build checkout
  `~/src/Sweetbriar`. I left it uncommitted and untouched.
- **Profiled retry:** the first profiled attempt launched behind Finder.
  I brought the app to the front about 2 minutes in, too late, and it
  failed with "the window wasn't key and the app active" and "the run
  lost the foreground". Its report is kept unmodified as
  `-profiled-invalid1.json`. The second attempt was brought to the
  front as soon as its process appeared, and passed.
- **Profiler issues:** `xctrace` reported "The log archive is corrupt or
  incomplete" on both profiled attempts and exited 2, but saved the
  trace. That leaves no signposts, so queries were located from the
  stacks. Sweetbriar frames needed re-symbolicating (`xctrace
  symbolicate` plus `atos`). Each `xctrace --launch` also left a
  stopped (`T`) copy of the app, which I killed after the recording.
- **Timed runs:** none lost the foreground and none were retried.
- **Kept on this Mac, not committed:** the traces, the raw XML exports
  and the logs are in `~/sb-bench-archive/20261029T212655Z/`. The
  first attempt's trace is under `profiled-attempt1/`.
